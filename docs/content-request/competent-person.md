# Content request — Competent person

For the competent person(s) (CP) who will review and **approve technical content** on the website. No CP is named
yet (Q-30). Read `README.md` first: every value needs a **source document** and an **as-at date**, and figures must
be given exactly as the source states them.

Unless a section says otherwise: **you verify and you approve (technical approval)**. "Current wording" is what the
old website said (captured 29 Sep 2026): **unverified**, some of it promotional, some of it wrong. Approve it as is,
rewrite it, or strike it; you are never asked to accept it.

**Launch column:** **B** blocks the whole launch · **P** blocks a specific page (named) · **D** desirable · **L** later.

A project page is published only when its holding, area and ownership are approved (`company-secretary.md` CS-1),
and any page showing exploration results, targets or resources must carry your competent-person statement and a
link to the disclaimer. Without a CP, project pages can show corporate facts only, and technical modules stay hidden.
